  1. 1 About this wiki

Welcome to the systemLinux Wiki: the documentation of systemLinux, a small source-based x86_64 GNU/Linux distribution with dinit instead of systemd and software built with get. Every page describes what systemLinux really does; when something is missing or not verified, the page says so.
